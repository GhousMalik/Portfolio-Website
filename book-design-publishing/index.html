Book Design & Publishing

Professional cover design and interior formatting for fiction and non-fiction — delivered as publishing-ready files that meet the technical specifications of Amazon KDP, IngramSpark and offset printers.

01

Amazon KDP Formatting

Paperback, hardcover and Kindle files set up to KDP trim, bleed and margin requirements.

02

IngramSpark Formatting

PDF/X-compliant interiors and covers prepared for IngramSpark distribution.

03

Paperback Book Formatting

Clean, professional paperback interiors in any standard trim size.

04

Hardcover Book Formatting

Case-laminate and dust-jacket layouts with correct board and hinge allowances.

05

Ebook Formatting

Reflowable and fixed-layout ebooks (EPUB / Kindle) that read well on every device.

06

Book Interior Layout

Chapter openers, headers, footers and page design that suit your genre.

07

Book Typesetting

Careful typography — widows, orphans, hyphenation and spacing handled properly.

08

Book Cover Design

Genre-aware front covers designed to stand out as a thumbnail and on the shelf.

09

Full Wrap Cover Design

Front, spine and back cover designed together, with blurb, author bio and barcode area.

10

Print-Ready PDF Preparation

CMYK conversion, font embedding and preflight checks for commercial printing.

11

Book Bleed and Margin Setup

Bleed, safe zones and gutter margins calculated for your page count and paper.

12

ISBN and Barcode Placement

ISBN barcode generated or placed to platform specifications.

13

Publishing-Ready Files

Final, organised files ready to upload — no back-and-forth with the printer.

14

Book Reformatting and Redesign

Refresh an existing title with a new cover, new interior or a new trim size.
