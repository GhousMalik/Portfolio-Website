About me

Designing books, brands and products for 12 years.

Graphic Designer | Book Designer | Illustrator | Publishing & Print Design Specialist

SAPlaceholder — add your portrait photo

Hello, I'm Sarosh.

I'm a freelance graphic designer, book designer and illustrator with approximately 12 years of experience in the design industry.

My work spans the full range of print and publishing design — children's book illustration, book covers and interior formatting for Amazon KDP and IngramSpark, magazine and catalog layout, and comic book art — alongside branding, marketing design, apparel graphics and embroidery patch artwork.

That breadth means I understand how a design behaves once it leaves the screen: how a spine width changes with page count, how colours shift in CMYK, how sublimation wraps around a seam, and how thread density affects a small patch. Clients get artwork that looks right and works in production.
