About

A designer who understands print, publishing and production.

I'm Sarosh Akhter Siddiqui, a freelance graphic designer, book designer and illustrator with around 12 years of experience in the design industry.

I work with independent and self-published authors, children's book authors, publishing companies and magazine publishers — as well as businesses, apparel and clothing brands, marketing agencies and entrepreneurs. Whether it's a picture book for Amazon KDP, a product catalog, a logo or a sublimated rashguard, I deliver artwork that's considered, consistent and ready for production.
