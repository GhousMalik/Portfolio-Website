FAQ

Common questions

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the form with your project details — for books, include page count, trim size and target platform. You’ll receive a quote and timeline by email.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I work with authors, publishers and businesses worldwide, communicating by email and WhatsApp across time zones.

Will my files be ready for Amazon KDP or IngramSpark?

Yes. Covers and interiors are prepared to each platform’s current specifications, including bleed, spine width and barcode placement.

Which file formats do you deliver?

Print-ready PDF, InDesign and Illustrator sources, vector files (AI, EPS, SVG), high-resolution PNG/JPG, EPUB/Kindle, and embroidery formats such as DST, EMB, PES, EXP, JEF and VP3.

How many revisions are included?

Revision rounds are agreed in the quote, so you know exactly what is included before the project begins.
